# TypeSafe Jev shadow trial

The owner-only panel reviews one player and prop at a time on the NHL preview. It asks three built-in choice questions about player opportunity, matchup, and data coverage. The owner selects a prop and player, then presses **Review with Jev**. No prompt writing is needed.

The panel supports Goals, Assists, Points, Shots, and Power Play. It sends only allowlisted pregame tracker fields for that market. Settled outcomes and secret values are excluded. It shows the fields sent, the choice labels, label confidence, and API token usage.

This trial does not change move rules, card ranking, prices, or tracker output. Jev's label confidence is not a prop hit probability. The labels need outcome grading before they can inform any board change.
